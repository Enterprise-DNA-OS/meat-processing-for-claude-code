# Research notes

Checked 19 November 2026.

## The incumbent

- Fujitsu MeatPro: "A proven, comprehensive and scalable cloud enabled platform built on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central", "specifically designed to meet the day to day needs and challenges of medium to large scale meat processing plants". Fujitsu "have implemented and maintained Fujitsu MeatPro for many Australian and New Zealand based meat processing plants for the last 20+ years". Covers procurement ("Detailed procurement costing, Flexible pricing for schedule/grid management"), sales, on-floor production data "transferred directly from the scales (weight and grade) or carton production systems (barcode)", and traceability through "the specialised Carton Master warehouse management system". Source: [Fujitsu Australia, archived 12 January 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260112171419/https://www.fujitsu.com/au/services/data-applications/enterprise-applications/connecting-with-microsoft/meatpro/index.html). The live page now redirects to Fujitsu's general enterprise applications page.
- No public price. Business Central itself is licensed per user per month (Microsoft's pricing page), plus the MeatPro layer and Fujitsu's implementation and support.
- Other systems plants use: Emydex (factory floor, rolled out across Silver Fern Farms' New Zealand plants), CSB-System, inecta and other Business Central meat add-ons.
